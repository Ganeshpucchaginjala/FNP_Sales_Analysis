# FNP Sales Analysis Dashboard (Excel)

## Overview
An interactive Excel dashboard built on 1,000 gifting orders to understand which occasions, categories, products and cities drive revenue, and how quickly orders are delivered.

## Objective
- Identify the top-performing occasions, categories, products and cities.
- Track revenue trends by month and by hour of order.
- Monitor delivery efficiency and average customer spend.

## Dataset
- 1,000 orders with order date and time, delivery date, occasion, category, product, city and revenue.

## Tools and Techniques
- Microsoft Excel: Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, Slicers, Timelines
- Excel formulas for calculated fields such as delivery time and revenue
- Data modelling to structure the data for analysis

## Key Metrics
| KPI | Value |
|---|---|
| Total Orders | 1,000 |
| Total Revenue | ₹35,20,984 |
| Avg Delivery Time | 5.53 days |
| Avg Customer Spend | ₹3,520.98 |

## Dashboard Features
- Revenue by occasion, category, month and order hour
- Top 5 products by revenue and top 10 cities
- Interactive filters: Order Date and Delivery Date timelines, Occasion slicer

## Key Insights
- Anniversary, Raksha Bandhan and Holi are the highest-revenue occasions.
- Colors is the top revenue category, followed by Soft Toys and Sweets.
- Revenue peaks in February and August.
